# Knee Recovery Tracker

A private, offline tracker for ACL / meniscus surgery recovery.

## Open it

Double-click `index.html` to open it in your browser. No install or internet needed.

Your data is saved in that browser on this computer. Always open it the same way, in the same browser: Safari and Chrome keep separate data, and so does opening the file directly vs. through a local server. Use **Settings → Export backup** now and then.

## What's in v1

- **Daily check-in**: everyday pain and pain during exercise/PT (both 0–10), swelling (0–10), flexion and extension degrees, walking status, brace, sleep (8:30 or 8.5), mood, icing, PT session, pain meds, notes. Log any past day too.
- **Exercises, logged per day**: tap quick-add buttons (most recently used first), and sets/reps fill in from the last time you did that exercise. "Repeat" copies your last session; type a new name to add something your PT just gave you, and it joins the quick-add list. Rate any exercise Easy / Moderate / Hard; Progress shows how each one has felt over time.
- **Steps**: entered as *yesterday's* total in each check-in, so you can log mid-day and still record a full day's steps.
- **Phase card**: post-op day and week, current rehab phase and its focus, and the next milestones coming up.
- **Progress**: stat cards (knee bend vs. a week ago, extension, 7-day pain trend, exercise completion, logging streak) and charts for flexion (with 90°/120°/135° targets and milestone markers), extension, pain and swelling, exercise days per week, and sleep.
- **Milestones**: 15 common ACL rehab milestones with typical week windows, plus your own. Check one off to record the date.
- **History**: every entry in a table, with edit and delete.
- **Settings**: surgery details, goal, an editable exercise library, JSON backup/restore, CSV export to share with your PT.

Typical timelines are general guides only. Follow your surgeon's and PT's protocol.
